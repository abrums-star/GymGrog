# Structured program and exercise additions (27 Nov 2026)

Why: after three weeks of real sessions the user wanted more exercises and more structure, picked from current evidence for their situation: fat loss, newly diagnosed high LDL cholesterol and borderline fasting sugar, a neglected back, surfing as the sport, early forties, irregular schedule around surf and a baby.

## What the sources say

**Cholesterol and fat.** Head-to-head trials in 2025 keep finding aerobic training moves LDL more than lifting, lifting moves it a little, and the combination does best. A 12-week trial in 45–69-year-olds with lipid or sugar problems used two aerobic and one resistance session a week and lowered LDL, weight and blood pressure. A meta-analysis of progressive resistance training reports a small LDL drop (about 5 %). So cardio is not an optional finisher for this user; it is half the point.
[Combined training, dyslipidemia](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0531556525002086) · [Aerobic vs resistance review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3906547/) · [Aerobic vs combined on LDL in men](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3721720/) · [Small circuits in men 35–40](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9457363/)

**Cardio intensity.** Steady "zone 2" work builds the base and fat oxidation; intervals are the time-efficient way to raise VO2max, which is the number most tied to longevity. The sensible mix for someone training under six hours a week is mostly steady with a few hard intervals. Many of the 2026 "zone 2" articles are marketing; the interval meta-analysis is the solid source.
[VO2max and interval training meta-analysis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3774727/) · [HIIT vs zone 2 summary](https://www.quanmed.ai/blog/hiit-vs-zone-2-vo2-max/)

**Periodization.** Three to six week blocks with a change of emphasis is coaching convention, not a tested threshold, but it is what every program-design source converges on. A build → strength → power sequence suits a surfer better than alternating hypertrophy and strength only, because pop-ups and take-offs are power.
[Block periodization guide](https://myliftingcoach.com/blog/block-periodization-guide) · [Periodization for natural lifters](https://humanfuelguide.com/en/articles/guides/periodization-basics) · [Sports periodization table](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4637911/table/T5)

**Surf-specific.** A systematic review of surfers with chronic shoulder complaints found reduced external rotation range and strength and recommends rotator cuff and upper-body training. Coaches add thoracic rotation (so the turn comes from the upper back, not the lower back), single-leg strength, paddling-pattern pulls (straight-arm pulldown, pull-ups, rows) and pop-up practice.
[Surfer's shoulder systematic review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40798-020-00289-0) · [Surf Strength Coach, shoulders](https://surfstrengthcoach.com/surf-shoulder-exercise/) · [Prehab Guys](https://theprehabguys.com/shoulder-strengthening-exercises-for-surfers/) · [Rye PT](https://ryephysicaltherapy.com/shoulder-health-for-the-surfer/) · [Surfolio plan](https://www.surfolio.com/surf-specific-strength-training-plan/) · [Mundo Surf 12 weeks](https://www.mundo-surf.com/en/news/surf-training/12-week-surf-training-program) · [Surfers Footprint prehab](https://www.surfersfootprint.com/the-unbreakable-surfer-injury-prevention-prehab-surf-workout-routine/)

## What changed in the app

1. **Three repeating 4-week phases** (`PHASES` in data.js), tied to the existing 28-day block counter:

   | Phase | Main lifts | Rest | Cardio finish | Extra |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Build | 3 × 12–15 | 60 s | 15 min steady | |
   | Strength | 3 × 8–10 | 120 s | 10 min steady | |
   | Power | 3 × 6–8 | 120 s | 8 min of 30 s hard / 90 s easy intervals | one pop-up power exercise per session |

   The circuit session C is the same in every phase. Rear delts, calves, leg isolation and core keep their own reps.

2. **Every session opens with** one round of mobility (thoracic rotation, cat-camel, hip flexor stretch or hip circles) and two sets of rotator-cuff work (band or cable external rotation, internal rotation, band pull-aparts).

3. **Suggestions follow the phase.** When the rep range changes by three or more reps, the suggested weight drops two steps (more reps) or rises one step (fewer reps), and the note says why.

4. **New exercises** (photos from free-exercise-db unless noted): straight-arm pulldown, pull-up (parked), inverted row, single-arm cable row, rope rear-delt row, reverse fly with external rotation, single-leg Romanian deadlift, barbell hip thrust (parked), dumbbell split squat, dumbbell step-up, single-leg glute bridge, incline dumbbell press, dip machine (parked), dumbbell shoulder press, box jump, medicine ball slam, medicine ball chest pass, plank to pop-up (no photo), plyo push-up (parked), plank with shoulder taps (no photo), push-up to side plank, stair climber, skipping rope (parked), bike intervals, rower intervals, plus the four mobility and four shoulder-health moves.

Not added on purpose: rotational core work (woodchop, Pallof press) and carries, which the user declined earlier. Rotation is covered by the thoracic mobility drill instead.
